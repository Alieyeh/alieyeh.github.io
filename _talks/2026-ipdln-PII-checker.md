Poster presented at IPDLN 2026 in Rotterdam, describing an NLP-driven tool for detecting and labelling potential participant identifiers in data handled within the Dementias Platform UK Data Portal.

The work supports:

- More consistent review of potentially identifying information
- Practical privacy checks for data entering secure research workflows
- Governance-aware tooling for research operations teams

The tool outcomes were also presented at HDR UK Conference 2025.
