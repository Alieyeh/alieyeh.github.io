**Status:** Technical manuscript in preparation.

This manuscript describes an end-to-end genomics data facilitation framework within the DPUK Data Portal, covering:

- Metadata-driven dataset discovery
- Structured QC and privacy screening workflows
- Standardised cohort organisation for scalable use
- Controlled and governed data provisioning
- Integration with HPC-based analytical workflows

The framework is designed to balance usability, reproducibility and governance in large-scale genomics research.
