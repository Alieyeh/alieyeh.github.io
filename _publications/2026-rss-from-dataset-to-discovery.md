**Status:** In editorial revision.

This follow-up article extends the five-pillar framework into applied study-design examples. It focuses on how dataset characteristics affect:

- GWAS design and interpretation
- PRS construction and portability
- Rare variant aggregation strategies
- Reproducibility, bias and feasibility

The aim is to help applied researchers connect dataset-selection decisions to the analyses they plan to run.
