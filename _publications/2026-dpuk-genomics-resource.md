**Status:** Resource manuscript in preparation.

This manuscript provides a structured overview of genomics data available within the Dementias Platform UK Trusted Research Environment. It is designed to help researchers understand what data exists, how it is structured and what needs to be considered before analysis.

The paper covers:

- Genomics datasets available through DPUK
- Cohort characteristics, genomic modalities and coverage
- Data readiness and harmonisation considerations
- Governance structures and access routes
- Metadata needed for transparent cohort selection

**Tags:** governance, data discovery, cohort selection, statistical summary, data exploration
