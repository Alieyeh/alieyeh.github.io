Published practical guide for researchers who need to choose genomic datasets before committing approvals, compute time and analysis effort.

The article translates a difficult technical and governance decision into a five-pillar framework:

- Data discovery and shortlisting
- Access, ethics and governance
- Technology and assay choice, including GWAS, WES and WGS
- Data content, phenotype depth, ancestry and cohort design
- Quality control, harmonisation and downstream analysis readiness

The guide is useful because it moves beyond listing resources. It helps researchers ask whether a dataset is fit for a specific question, and why that choice affects validity, bias, feasibility and reproducibility.
