This published practical guide helps researchers who are new to genomics make better dataset-selection decisions before they commit time, approvals or compute resources.

The article is especially useful because it translates a difficult technical and governance-heavy decision into a structured five-pillar framework:

- Data discovery and shortlisting
- Access, ethics and governance
- Technology and assay choice, including GWAS, WES and WGS
- Data content, phenotype depth, ancestry and cohort design
- Quality control, harmonisation and downstream analysis readiness

Rather than simply listing data resources, the guide explains how to evaluate whether a dataset is fit for a specific research question. It highlights why dataset choice shapes scientific validity, reproducibility, bias, feasibility and responsible data use.
