Hotchkiss L., Adeoeye K., **Sarabandi Moghaddam A.**, Squires E., Thompson S. (2024).

Workshop report on synthetic data and privacy within healthcare research environments. The output summarises practical challenges, threat models and implementation considerations for privacy-preserving synthetic data generation.

My contribution was informed by hands-on experiments comparing models trained with real and synthetic dementia data and assessing their exposure to privacy attacks.
