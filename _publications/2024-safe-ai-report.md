Hotchkiss L., Squires E., Adeoye K., **Sarabandi Moghaddam A.**, Heys S., Golightly E., Morris C., Rittman T., Gallacher J., Thompson S. (2024).

Policy and technical report examining the risks of AI development and model release within sensitive healthcare Trusted Research Environments. The report covers privacy attack vectors, governance approaches and practical mitigation strategies for responsible model development and access.

My technical contribution included developing dementia classification models, comparing safer and baseline variants, testing synthetic-data approaches, prototyping a homomorphic-encryption-compatible route, and running membership, likelihood, worst-case, attribute and structural privacy assessments. These experiments informed the report's recommendations on model release and secure access.
